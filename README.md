
# TrialTrack – Clinical Trial Management System

## Project Overview
TrialTrack is a web-based clinical trial site management and quality tracking application inspired by the life sciences technology industry.

It is designed to help clinical research teams organize research sites, monitor documentation, track issues, and manage important tasks from one centralized dashboard.

## Industry Challenge
Clinical research teams handle large amounts of documentation, site information, and study-related tasks. Managing these activities manually can make it difficult to identify missing documents, monitor unresolved issues, and maintain visibility across research sites.

TrialTrack aims to address this challenge through a centralized digital management system.

## Key Features
- Dashboard with study and site statistics
- Research site management
- Clinical document tracking
- Issue reporting and resolution
- Task assignment and deadline tracking
- Activity and audit history
- User authentication and role-based access
